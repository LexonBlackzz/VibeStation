Startup timeline

  • 0.0–4.4 s vs2start.mp4 boot animation, full screen.
  • 4.5 s video turns black. Dust specks fade in on the black.
  • 4.9 s orbs fly out from the centre onto the ring, one every 120 ms.
  • 5.9 s menu items slide in from the right, then the hints.
  • 12.76 s video and audio end. The UI is already usable, so the user can skip the tail.

Building it in ImGui

  • Orbit: orbs on a tilted 3D ring with perspective, drawn far to near with ImDrawList: additive halo and core sprites, tapered tail segments and a twinkling cross flare, same approach as definitive_background.cpp.
  • Motion: a 30 s cycle from the reveal. The ring holds and locks in (0–3 s), pulls into one big orb (3–5.5 s), holds (5.5–7.5 s), drifts apart to each orb's own tilted orbit (7.5–15 s), roams the sphere (15–23 s), then gathers back into the ring (23–30 s). Positions are blends of a ring slot and an own-orbit point, scaled toward the centre by the merge weight.
  • Menu: definitive_home.cpp's item list, re-laid out as a carousel with eased y and alpha.
  • Sound: vs2-highlight on moving the selection, vs2-menuopen / vs2-menuclose on entering or leaving a screen, vs2-selected on confirming an action. They hook into the existing definitive_audio.cpp sound-effect path.
  • Video: decode in the intro module (pl_mpeg for MPEG-1, or Media Foundation for the existing .mp4) and start the reveal at the first fully black frame.
